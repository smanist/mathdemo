# Double Pendulum Simulation

This page computes the motion for the
same parameter set: gravity, the two rod lengths, and
the two bob masses.

The initial conditions are fixed at
$\theta_1(0)=120^\circ$, $\theta_2(0)=-10^\circ$, and
$\dot{\theta}_1(0)=\dot{\theta}_2(0)=0$.

This page shows the evolving motion with a trailing path.
